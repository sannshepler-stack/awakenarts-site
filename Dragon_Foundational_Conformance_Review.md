# Dragon Figure Edition — Foundational Conformance Review

**Document type:** Not a Larger Story revision, and not an eighth revision of anything. Per Susan's own naming, this is a distinct process from `Dragon_Christian_Fulfillment_Review.md`: the question here is not "what Scripture fits Dragon" but *"does this founding edition now embody the philosophy it gave birth to?"* Every page, cover to colophon, read fresh — as if encountered for the first time — against the completed `AwakenArts_Editorial_Philosophy.md` and `AwakenArts_Figure_Edition_Production_Standard.md`.

**Method.** Each page below is read against: the vocation (prepare the imagination — not merely art, not merely discussion, not merely doctrine); Recognition and Truth (is this true, and is it faithful to Christ and Scripture); the five-part movement (symbolic story awakens recognition → Scripture reveals → tradition illuminates → Christ remains the center → reader invited to live in that reality); the Parable and Bridge Principles; the Dual Standard (literary completeness through Recognition; theological faithfulness at and after The Larger Story); the Editorial Voice checklist (invite, observe, recognize, ask, remain literary); Preserve Mystery; and Reader-Centered Language.

**Finding, stated up front, per Susan's own suspicion:** The Larger Story was not the only page needing attention. Three residual vocabulary inconsistencies survive from earlier drafts — all predating or bypassed by the seven Larger Story revisions, because those revisions only ever looked at page 6 itself. None are visible from inside page 6. All three are found on pages the Larger Story work never touched.

## Page 5 · Recognition

**Text in full:** Quote ("The dragon didn't seem to know tails and heads are one somehow") → Observe → 01 Explore the Pattern → 02 Develop the Insight → 03 Return to the Image.

**Conformance:** Strong, with one finding.

- **Observe** and **01** hold up fully: "a single creature whose nature has simply not yet been reconciled within itself" and "mistook their difference for a battle to be won, rather than a wholeness to be claimed" both use the edition's corrected vocabulary (reconciled, wholeness) and match Colossians 3's own logic (dissolution of false opposition, not defeat of one side).
- **03** is already fully corrected — "A wholeness like this is never self-made. It is received before it is lived" — the working example of grace carried without the word "grace," cited approvingly in the Editorial Philosophy itself.
- **Finding — 02 still says "peace":** *"The dragon's rage is not the enemy of its peace — it is the part of the dragon still waiting to be heard."* This predates, and was never touched by, the "peace" → "wholeness" correction applied to 03 and to Living the Message. It is the edition's own descriptive vocabulary (not a Scripture quotation), so the standing correction applies: peace is the result Dragon's language describes elsewhere, wholeness is the movement. **Recommend:** "The dragon's rage is not the enemy of its wholeness —" or equivalent, matching 03's own word exactly.

I considered whether Observe's declarative voice ("The dragon in this image is not one thing fighting another. It is one body...") should become more question-based to match "ask rather than persuade." Concluded no: Section 2 deliberately separates Recognition (where the pattern is named, in prose) from Reflection (where it turns into private open questions) — the declarative register here is functioning as designed, not drifting from it.

## Page 6 · The Larger Story

**Text:** Current seventh-revision draft (Colossians 3:10–11, 14–15; Lewis; Nouwen; Returning to the Dragon).

**Conformance:** Strong — this is the page that has already absorbed seven rounds of correction against exactly these criteria. Confirmed again here: Scripture is the center of gravity, the figure is never treated as wisdom/gospel/biblical character, the closing paragraph hands the final beat to the reader, and the five-part movement is present in substance.

- **One live, not-yet-resolved opportunity, carried forward from `Dragon_Christian_Tradition_Research.md`:** neither the opening frame line ("Christian Scripture and tradition have long spoken of this divided human condition...") nor the Scripture intro line ("The old grounds for opposition... do not survive in the One who holds every part together") names *why* the false opposition arises in the first place. Susan's own recent insight — the human heart is disordered by sin, which is why we so often fight the wrong battles — supplies exactly this missing "why," and would deepen this page's fidelity to Recognition and Truth without disturbing its literary register, if it can be worked in without becoming didactic. Recorded as a recommendation, not applied here, consistent with the standing caution against moving this theological layer at ordinary editing speed.

## Page 7 · Reflection

**Text in full:** Intro line → "Where Am I Fighting Myself?" → "What Part of Me Have I Called the Enemy?" → "What Would 'And' Look Like Here?"

**Conformance:** Two findings — the most significant of this review.

- **Finding — intro line references a Scripture that no longer exists on the page it's citing:** *"Let both the dragon's story and the peace named on the page before this one sit alongside your own life as you answer."* This line was written when The Larger Story's Scripture was Ephesians 2:14 ("For he is our peace, who hath made both one...") — the very first, since-superseded draft. It was never updated through any of the six subsequent revisions, because none of those revisions looked outside page 6. The Larger Story no longer "names peace" as its point — it names the dissolution of old oppositions in Christ ("Christ is all, and in all") and love as "the bond of perfectness." **Recommend:** reword to reflect what the page now actually says — for example, "Let both the dragon's story and what Scripture and the Christian tradition just named sit alongside your own life as you answer" — rather than repeating a word the current page doesn't foreground.
- **Finding — second prompt also uses "peace" as the edition's own vocabulary:** *"The part of you that feels too much, wants too much, or resists too much may not be the obstacle to your peace."* Same standing correction applies. **Recommend:** "...may not be the obstacle to your wholeness."
- The third prompt ("What Would 'And' Look Like Here?") and its body text are already fully consistent — no change needed there.

## Summary Against the Dual Standard

**Level 1 — does the edition stand as a complete literary work through Recognition?** Yes, with one word-level fix recommended (page 5). Nothing structural is missing; a reader who stopped at Recognition would have a complete, self-sufficient symbolic encounter.

**Level 2 — does The Larger Story faithfully receive that work into the truth revealed in Christ?** Yes, as already established across seven revisions. One enrichment opportunity noted (naming sin as the reason for the false opposition), not a failure of the existing text.

## Findings, Consolidated

1. Page 5 — "peace" → "wholeness" (vocabulary consistency; small, mechanical).
2. Page 7 — intro line's "the peace named on the page before this one" is a stale reference to the superseded Ephesians 2:14 draft; needs rewording to match the current Larger Story.
3. Page 7 — second prompt's "obstacle to your peace" → "obstacle to your wholeness" (same standing correction).
4. Page 6 — opportunity, not a defect: naming sin as the reason for the divided condition, if it can be done without becoming didactic.
5. Page 9 — the optional Christian-fulfillment addition remains an open decision, worth revisiting now, not a gap in the current text.

Everything else — Pages 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 10, 11, and the majority of Pages 5, 6, and 7 — conforms fully to the completed philosophy as written. This confirms Susan's own expectation stated going into this review: refinement, not reinvention. The two vocabulary corrections (1 and 3) are mechanical and low-risk. The reworded transition (2) needs actual drafting. Items 4 and 5 are decisions for Susan, not conformance failures.

**Status:** Review complete. No changes have been made to the PDF, the Good Copy docx, or any rendered asset. Presented for Susan's direction on which findings to act on before any page is touched.
