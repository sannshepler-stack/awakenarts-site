### Item 4 — Copyright Placement (Page 4)

**Decision:** Reject

**Implementation:** No revision. Retain the author's name and copyright notation with the poem. Continue to include the formal copyright statement on the Colophon page.

**Editorial Rationale:** The author's name and copyright notation are intentionally incorporated into the presentation of the poem as part of the artist's signature. Both should remain.

**Standards amendment required:** This decision supersedes the clause in Standard 15 of `Figure_Edition_Standard_Dragon.md` that reads: *"The copyright symbol shall not appear both within the poem and in the Colophon — choose one location for the legal notice."* Standard 15 must be revised to reflect that poem attribution and Colophon copyright serve distinct purposes and both are standard. ### Item 7 — Observe Section Description (Page 6)

**Decision:** Accept with Significant Revision
