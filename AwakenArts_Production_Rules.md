# AwakenArts — Production Rules

**Status: Governing reference. Issued 2026-07-01.**

Production Rules govern the classification and handling of artwork as it enters the AwakenArts production system. They exist above any individual production standard — they determine which standard applies before production begins.

---

## Production Rule No. 1 — Edition Art Prints vs. Sketchbook Works

Do not confuse Edition Art Prints with Sketchbook Works. These are two separate product families with different purposes, production standards, assets, and customer experiences.

### Edition Art Prints

- Originate from a completed Figure Edition.
- Exist as collectible fine art.
- Lead to fine art print purchase pages.
- Standard offering: 8 × 10 archival print. Additional sizes available through print-on-demand upon request.
- Every Edition Art Print remains explicitly connected to its originating Figure Edition.
- **Guiding question:** Would someone want to display this artwork on a wall?

### Sketchbook Works

- Originate as original artwork by Susan Ann Shepler.
- Are independent of the Figure Edition production system.
- Currently focused on *A Collection of Feminine Motifs*.
- Lead to greeting card purchase pages.
- Products include downloadable greeting cards and print-on-demand greeting cards, with future paper products as appropriate.
- **Guiding question:** Would someone want to send, share, or gift this artwork?

### Production Discipline

Whenever a new artwork enters production, the first decision is: **Is this an Edition Art Print or a Sketchbook Work?**

That decision determines the entire production path. No production standards, file naming, asset preparation, purchase pages, or product recommendations should cross between these two product families unless Susan explicitly directs otherwise.

When reviewing or producing artwork, never assume a work belongs to one product family because it resembles another. Classification is determined by its intended role within the AwakenArts architecture, not by its visual style.

## Production Rule No. 2 — Website rooms are presentation spaces, not product families

Product families determine production standards. Website rooms determine customer navigation.

Never classify a product according to the page where it appears. The Gallery is a room — it displays Edition Art Prints. An Edition Art Print is a product family — it has its own production standards, assets, file naming, review checklist, and purchase path. These two things share an architectural relationship but they are not the same thing.

This rule exists because the room names at AwakenArts (Gallery, Collection, Sketchbook, Encounters) are also common nouns that sound like product categories. They are not. The vocabulary is distinct:

- **Gallery** — the website room at `/gallery`. A presentation space. Displays Edition Art Prints. No purchase links.
- **Edition Art Print** — the product family. A collectible fine art print derived from a published Figure Edition. Has a purchase page.
- **Collection** — the website room at `/collection`. Displays Figure Editions by contact sheet. Leads to Edition Preview.
- **Sketchbook** — the website room at `/sketchbook`. Displays Sketchbook Works. Leads to individual work pages.

When in doubt: rooms have URLs. Product families have production paths.

## Production Rule No. 4 — Every commercial product must originate from a published work

AwakenArts does not create products to fill a catalog. It creates products because a completed work naturally lends itself to another form.

A greeting card exists because an artwork deserves to become a greeting card — not because the catalog needs more cards. A fine art print exists because a figure was painted and deserves to be seen on a wall. A book exists because a body of work has accumulated enough to support one.

No product enters the AwakenArts production system unless a completed, authored work is its origin. Commerce is the mechanism of distribution; the work is the reason for distribution.
