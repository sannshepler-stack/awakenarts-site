# The AwakenArts Figure Edition Series

**Status: Governing series introduction. Issued 2026-07-06.** This is the standing, quotable introduction to the Figure Edition collection — the front door, not a standard. `AwakenArts_Editorial_Philosophy.md` explains why the series is built the way it is; `AwakenArts_Figure_Edition_Production_Standard.md` explains how; this document is what to hand someone meeting the series for the first time.

---

Each Figure Edition invites readers into a symbolic story that becomes a way of recognizing the patterns that shape ordinary life.

A Figure Edition is not an art book, a poetry collection, a workbook, or a study guide, though it shares something with each of them. It begins with a single original figure — an image and a poem, encountered before anything is explained. From there, the edition guides a reader through recognition, reflection, and journaling, and closes by handing the reader back to their own life with something to keep noticing.

The series is not a set of separate products loosely bearing the same name. Every edition moves the reader through the same arc — encounter, notice, recognize, reflect, respond, return to ordinary life — and closes the same way: not with an explanation, but with an invitation to keep recognizing the pattern after the book is shut. What changes from one edition to the next is the figure and its story. What stays constant is what a Figure Edition *does*.

Each edition stands complete on its own: an individual can read it alone, a small group can use it together without a facilitator, and a workshop leader can go further with the companion Leader's Resource built for that edition. None of those uses require the others.

**The editions.** The Dragon is the series' founding edition — the working prototype against which the publishing language, reader journey, and design standard for every edition that follows are tested. Grismere, Bowls, Poppy, Queen Ann, and Ballerina follow, each built to the same standard, each its own symbolic encounter.
