## Component-by-Component Classification

### 1. The Dragon Edition prototype (11-page participant PDF)

**Overall: KEEP the content, REFRAME the delivery form.**

This is the strongest asset in the entire system, and the New Paradigm document already knows it — it explicitly instructs building the workshop on top of "Dragon's central conflict: its fire lands on the town it believes it is defending" and "Not This or That, But And." Both live here, fully realized, theologically vetted across seven revision passes (per the Foundational Conformance Review), and load-bearing for the new arc's stage 5, **Disruption**.

Page by page:

- **Cover / Begin** ("Be still. Let the figure arrive before the explanation does.") — **KEEP outright.** This is already Orientation-without-giving-away-the-reveal, word for word what the new arc's stage 1 asks for. Preserve mystery about the discovery, not the purpose — already true here.
- **The Image / The Poem** — **KEEP as raw material, STAGE the delivery.** Currently a static print spread. The new arc's stages 2–4 (Encounter, Association, Story/Poem) want this experienced as a sequence with room for participants to react before more is revealed — the content is ready; the presentation format (a page you turn) needs to become a paced reveal (a slide sequence with held silence).
- **Recognition** (Observe → Explore the Pattern → Develop the Insight → Return to the Image) — **REFRAME.** This is already close: "Observe" is object-level, "Develop the Insight" moves toward meaning. But it is written as declarative prose *for the reader to read*, not as a question sequence *for a facilitator to ask live*. Per the Foundational Conformance Review this section is otherwise in strong shape (one small "peace"→"wholeness" vocabulary fix outstanding) — the content survives, the mode of delivery changes.
- **The Larger Story** (Scripture/theology page) — **KEEP, position later.** This is the Edition's theological anchor and does not need paradigm-driven changes to its content. Its placement in the new arc is a sequencing question, not a content question (see Structural Problems — the org already has an unresolved internal debate, flagged in the *Encounter Journal* conformance review, about Scripture-before-prompts vs. Scripture-after-prompts; the same tension applies here and should be decided once, not per-edition).
- **Reflection** (Where Am I Fighting Myself? / What Part of Me Have I Called the Enemy? / What Would "And" Look Like Here?) — **REFRAME toward explicit meta-level framing.** These are good, personally relevant questions, but they ask *what the participant recognizes*, not *what the participant's mind just did*. The New Paradigm's stage 6 (Meta Shift) wants an explicit intermediate step — "What did you initially believe was happening? What did you supply?" — before landing on these. Recommend treating the existing three questions as the **Recognition** stage (7) they already resemble, and writing new, genuinely meta-level questions for stage 6.
- **Journal** — **KEEP.** Already functions as the new arc's stage 8 (Conscious Language): "let it stay open, the way recognition usually works" is precisely "writing allows recognition to become conscious language."
- **Message Delivered / Living the Message** — **KEEP, extend into Application.** Strong closing material, already resolves "not this or that, but and" as the thesis. What it doesn't yet do is ask the new arc's stage 9 questions explicitly (What do I want to do with this? Is this something I want to continue carrying forward?) — currently the Edition ends on recognition and image, not on a forward-looking choice. **MISSING** an explicit Application beat.
- **About This Edition** — **KEEP as-is**; colophon, unaffected by any of this.

### 2. The Dragon Companion (`Dragon_Companion_Good_Copy.docx`, 21 pages)

**Overall: MOVE roughly 55%, REFRAME roughly 25%, MISSING the rest.**

The system-review agent's page-by-page accounting is the clearest lens here:

- **Facilitator-only material (Discussion Objectives, six Group Questions, Teaching Cautions, the full nine-stage Workshop Plan with script) — 6 of 21 pages, already explicitly marked "NOT FOR PARTICIPANT DISTRIBUTION."** This is content that belongs in a Facilitator Guide by the New Paradigm's own definition and is **already separated out and ready to MOVE almost verbatim.** One caveat: the Teaching Cautions are written in the imperative "do not" register the org's own Precedent Standard has already flagged as needing a voice pass ("Do not let recognition stand in for reconciliation" → "Recognition is not being asked to do the work of reconciliation"). REFRAME the voice, keep the substance.
- **Learning section (4 pages: "A Battle the Dragon Did Not Choose," "Rage as a Revealer, Not an Enemy," "The Word That Reconciles," "Message Delivered") — REFRAME/STAGE, this is priority work.** This is genuinely the best-written explanatory material in the system (the "rage as revealer, not enemy" reversal is a real insight, sourced to depth psychology per the prior system review), and it is also the single clearest example of point 1 in Susan's brief: **"where Dragon currently explains rather than creates an experience."** It is essay prose meant to be read or spoken aloud. Under the new arc, this content is exactly what stages 5–7 (Disruption, Meta Shift, Recognition) need to *reveal through staged discovery* rather than *state as conclusion*. Recommend treating this section as the intellectual outline for those three stages, not as slide copy.
- **Encounter + Reflection (participant-facing, ~9 pages)** — **KEEP the Reflection questions (see above, REFRAME toward meta-level); MISSING the Encounter content entirely.** Two unresolved production gaps flagged directly in the Good Copy itself: the poem text is illegible in the only source file located, and no confirmed final artwork file exists. **This is a MISSING item independent of the paradigm shift** — it blocks Encounter regardless of which workshop model is used, and should be resolved early since Encounter is stage 2 of the new arc and the literal first thing a participant sees.
- **A tracked-then-lost inconsistency, worth surfacing now rather than after rebuilding:** `Dragon_Companion_Manuscript_v1.docx` explicitly flagged that the Companion's Learning section cites Ephesians 2:14/16 while the finished Edition's Larger Story settled on a different citation, and that Learning's "fulfills his duty" doesn't match the Edition's "brings a message discovered through struggle." Good Copy carries the inconsistency forward but drops the tracking note admitting it exists. **This should be resolved before any Learning content is repurposed into the new workshop**, or it will get built into two new documents at once.

### 3. Facilitator notes and the comprehensive facilitation document

**Overall: KEEP the philosophy wholesale; MISSING the delivery-craft layer entirely; REFRAME nothing — this material simply needs a new companion piece, not a rewrite.**

`AwakenArts_Guide_to_Symbolic_Facilitation.pdf` is the org's strongest single asset for **point 9** of Susan's brief ("what the presenter will need"). Its core stance — "presence without rescue, curiosity without certainty, companionship without authorship," the laddering technique (Observation → Association → Feeling → Meaning), the resistance/silence/disclosure handling in Chapters 7 and 11 — is exactly the posture the New Paradigm wants, and it predates the New Paradigm document. This is a **KEEP**, full stop, and should be treated as the philosophical bedrock under whatever Facilitator Guide gets built for Dragon specifically.

What it does not have, and what the New Paradigm explicitly requires (Presenter Requirement: "would I want to keep watching this if nobody required me to?"), is **any treatment of engagement, pacing, energy, or entertainment craft.** The Guide's register is calm, literary, restraint-oriented — closer to a clinical-supervision manual than a presentation-design document. This is a clean **MISSING**, not a flaw in what exists: the Guide was never trying to solve this problem, and shouldn't be edited to pretend it did. It needs a new, short, entertainment/engagement-craft layer written specifically for the presenter's side of a live room, sitting alongside the Guide rather than inside it.

Two further, more mechanical findings that any Facilitator Guide build needs to resolve first:

- **Stage-count inconsistency across the org's own governing documents.** The Guide's Recognition Model has 4 movements. The Facilitator Resource Brief specifies 6 stages / 7 resource sections. The Dragon Workshop Plan cites a 9-stage "Workshop Presentation Standard." The New Paradigm document proposes 10. None of the first three are reconciled with each other today, and the New Paradigm's 10 should be understood as the superseding model going forward — but this needs to be stated explicitly somewhere, or the next builder will inherit four contradictory stage counts with no crosswalk.
- **Two of the Dragon Workshop Plan's nine stages (Welcome, Orientation) have zero scripted content** — only a timing row. Everything else in the plan has at least one verbatim cue line. This is the most concrete **MISSING** item standing between the current material and "a novice presenter... without requiring her to become a conventional lecturer."

### 4. The Companion architecture documents (Brief vs. Precedent Standard)

**REMOVE the contradiction, not the content.** `AwakenArts_Facilitator_Companion_Brief.docx` describes two fully separate products (Participant Guide, Facilitator Companion, never in the same hands). `AwakenArts_Companion_Precedent_and_Template_Standard.md` — written a month later, naming Dragon as its own working precedent — describes one bound document with embedded, detachable participant pages, which is what actually got built. These two documents govern the same territory and disagree with each other. Since the New Paradigm document now separates "Participant Presentation," "Facilitator Guide," and "Participant Material" into three distinct deliverables (its section VI), it implicitly resolves this in the Brief's direction — worth stating that decision explicitly and retiring or marking the Brief as superseded, so the next person building a Companion for Bowls, Ballerina, or Poppy isn't handed two contradictory standards.

### 5. The website: Editions / Collection / contact sheets

**Overall: the funnel is REMOVE; the content underneath it is KEEP; the connective tissue is MISSING.**

The current live path — Collection grid → contact sheet → `/editions/[slug]` ("Acquire" link) → `/editions/[slug]/purchase` (disabled button, "Price to be announced," noindexed) — is a dead end for every visitor who completes it, and it is the site's most direct expression of exactly the old model the New Paradigm discontinues ("Editions as facilitator products"). This funnel should be **REMOVED** as a destination, though the contact-sheet-as-visual-trailer idea survives, per the New Paradigm's own section VII: "The contact sheet functions almost like a visual trailer. It should make someone curious enough to enter."

Two findings make this more tractable than a from-scratch rebuild:

It is currently **orphaned** — not linked from the Collection, the edition preview page, or anywhere else in the UI, reachable only by typing the URL directly. This is close to a **KEEP**, if its content is reframed per the notes above (Section 1) and it's positioned as *part of a workshop's Participant Presentation*, not as a stand-in for the old sales page.
- **Five of six Editions have empty content (`sections: []`)** in the Reader — a genuine **MISSING**, but bounded: Dragon is the one edition that's actually finished, which is exactly why the New Paradigm document proposes using Dragon to prove the system before touching the others.

### 6. The website: `/workshops` and `/facilitator-orientation`

### 7. The website: `/encounters/dragon`

## Answering Susan's Twelve Points Directly

1. **Where Dragon currently explains rather than creates an experience** — primarily the Companion's Learning section (essay prose delivering the "rage as revealer" and "word that reconciles" insights as conclusions rather than reveals) and the Edition's Recognition page (declarative "Observe" prose rather than a live question sequence).
2. **Where existing material already creates genuine curiosity, discovery, recognition, or surprise** — the Edition's "Begin" page ("let the figure arrive before the explanation does"), the Companion's own Encounter-stage instruction ("do not explain the symbolism beforehand... do not name 'animus,' 'shadow,' or 'and' during this stage"), and above all Dragon's own plot mechanism — his fire lands on the town he believes he's defending — which is a real, built-in surprise, not a stated concept.
3. **Where object-level material can lead naturally into meta-level awareness** — the Recognition and Reflection pages already gesture this direction but stop at "what does this mean to me," short of "what did my mind just do." This is the clearest, most contained REFRAME opportunity in the whole system.
4. **Where "Not This or That, But And" can become experiential discovery rather than a stated concept** — this is Dragon's actual story mechanic (fire falls on the town he's defending), already present, currently *told* on the Cover as a thesis line before the discovery happens. Moving the phrase to a *late* reveal rather than an opening thesis would let participants arrive at it themselves.
5. **Where poetry and imagery are integral rather than decorative** — the Dragon poem and the Companion Artwork Brief's reject list (no cartoonish dragons, no combat-pose violence, no creature-feature monsters) show real discipline protecting the figure from becoming decoration. This is sound and should be preserved as-is.
6. **Where reflective writing can allow recognition to become conscious language** — the Journal page already does this well; the Reflection questions need the meta-level reframe noted above to earn their place in the new arc's stage 8 rather than stage 7.
7. **Where the participant currently lacks a clear WHY or practical connection** — the Edition ends on Living the Message without an explicit Application beat (What do I want to do with this? Is this something I want to keep carrying forward?). This is a genuine MISSING, not present anywhere in the current 11 pages.
8. **Where entertainment, pacing, reveal, story, curiosity needs strengthening** — everywhere the content is currently a page to be read rather than a sequence to be experienced live: Recognition, Learning, and the transition from Encounter into Reflection all need staging, not more words.
9. **What the presenter needs to conduct each part confidently** — a moment-by-moment script for Welcome and Orientation (currently blank), an engagement/pacing layer the Guide doesn't cover, and one clean, reconciled stage model instead of four competing ones.
10. **What the website does well vs. merely displays** — does well: the homepage's sequenced arc, the Symbols lightbox, the Encounters pacing philosophy. Merely displays: the entire Collection → Edition → Purchase funnel, which is attractive and dead-ended at once.
11. **How contact sheets can transition from product previews to workshop invitations** — the New Paradigm's own language already answers this ("the contact sheet functions almost like a visual trailer"); mechanically, this means retiring the Purchase page, repointing the "Acquire" link, and connecting Collection to `/workshops` and to a repositioned `EditionReader`.
12. **What's common to every workshop vs. specific to Dragon's world** — common: the ten-stage arc, the object/meta distinction, the Guide's posture and safety material, `/workshops`' six-step rhythm and format taxonomy. Dragon-specific: the poem, the artwork, "Not This or That, But And," the rage-as-revealer insight, the Scripture citations.

2. **The Facilitator Notes' closing line** — "the dragon is not the obstacle to the story. The instinct behind Dragon's development was already pointed the right direction.
3. **`AwakenArts_Guide_to_Symbolic_Facilitation.pdf`** — the posture, the laddering technique, and the safety/scope-of-practice material are complete, sound, and require no paradigm-driven changes.
4. **`/workshops` and `/facilitator-orientation`** — fully built, philosophically aligned, currently invisible. The single highest-leverage discovery of this audit: most of "build a workshops page" is already done and simply needs to be connected and surfaced.

## B. Structural Problems

What currently prevents this system from functioning as the newly defined workshop experience:

1. **Four unreconciled stage models** across the Guide (4), the Facilitator Resource Brief (6/7), the Dragon Workshop Plan (9), and the New Paradigm document (10) — no crosswalk exists anywhere.
2. **Two contradictory Companion architecture standards** (the Brief's strict two-product separation vs. the Precedent Standard's one-document-with-embedded-pages model, which is what was actually built).
3. **A dead-end commerce funnel** (Collection → Edition → disabled Purchase page) sitting exactly where a workshop invitation needs to go instead.
4. **Orphaned high-value assets** — `EditionReader.tsx` and `/workshops`/`/facilitator-orientation` are all built and unlinked from the paths a real visitor follows.
5. **An unresolved tonal contradiction** between the Encounters page's adversarial Scripture framing (James 4:7, "resist the devil") and the Companion's explicit reversal ("not a story about defeating a monster") — flagged once already, never resolved.
6. **A tracked-then-lost content inconsistency** (Ephesians 2:14/16 vs. the Edition's actual citation; "fulfills his duty" vs. "brings a message discovered through struggle") that was flagged in the Companion's v1 manuscript and silently dropped from Good Copy without being fixed.
7. **Two blocking production gaps** in the Companion's Encounter section (illegible poem source, no confirmed artwork file) that block Encounter regardless of which paradigm governs the workshop.
8. **No engagement/entertainment/pacing layer anywhere** in the org's facilitation materials, general or Dragon-specific — a real gap against the New Paradigm's explicit requirement.
9. **No moment-by-moment content for two of nine Workshop Plan stages** (Welcome, Orientation) — the most concrete gap standing between current material and "novice presenter" readiness.

## G. Recommended Goal 2

**Build the Dragon Facilitator Guide's stage-by-stage script and the missing Application/Meta-Shift content — in that order, before touching any slide or web page.**

Its job is to prove the system." A test run will also surface whether the ten-stage arc holds up in real time better than any further desk analysis could.
