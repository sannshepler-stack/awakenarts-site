# AwakenArts — Figure Research and Development Standard

**Status: Governing correction. Issued 2026-08-20, per Susan Ann Shepler's direct instruction. Governs how every Figure — Dragon and all Figures still to come — is developed from this point forward.**

---

## The Correction

AwakenArts has, at points, treated a Figure's symbolic content as a scaffold for participant reflection prompts — generating "what do you notice" / "what does this bring to mind" questions and building workshop content outward from there. That approach is retired.

A Figure is not a prompt. It is a symbolic field: a poem, an image, a body of language, and a set of cultural, literary, and scriptural associations that already carry meaning before any participant enters the room.

**Facilitation does not add depth to a Figure. Facilitation helps a participant encounter and work with depth that is already there, because the research has already been done.**

---

## The Standard

Before any workshop content — script, slide, question, or exercise — is written for a Figure, that Figure must be researched and understood as a symbolic field:

- **The poem.** Read closely: form, image, turn, what it withholds, what it declares.
- **Language.** The etymology and connotation of the Figure's own name and central images — what the words carry that a casual reading would miss.
- **Cultural and literary associations.** How this figure/symbol/image has been used elsewhere — myth, literature, art, Scripture, tradition — and where AwakenArts' use agrees with, departs from, or complicates that inheritance.
- **The human pattern it embodies.** What actual, recognizable human experience this Figure gives symbolic form to — not "what feeling does this evoke," but "what pattern of being human is this the shape of."

Workshop development begins only once that substance is established, and is *developed from* that substance — not assembled from a generic pacing template with the Figure's name dropped in.

## How the Developmental Movement Is Found, Not Imposed

*Correction, 2026-08-20, per Susan's direct instruction:* AwakenArts does not impose a self-development process upon symbolic works. It works with the developmental movement that symbolic and archetypal material itself can reveal — from what lies beyond awareness toward recognition, understanding, integration, wholeness, and connection.

This qualifies Steps 2 and 3 below. Researching the facilitation method (Step 2) is not a search for an external developmental template to select and apply — it is a search for methods capable of *supporting* whatever movement the Figure's own poem, image, and symbolic field (Step 1) already reveal. Understanding the poem/image (Step 3) comes first and governs; a facilitation method's own stage structure — VTS's developmental levels, Kolb's four-stage cycle, a workshop's own prior stage architecture — is a resource that may illuminate that movement. It is never a mold the Figure is poured into.

In practice: when a general framework's shape turns out to resemble a Figure's own revealed movement — as `AwakenArts_Facilitation_Method_Research.md` found with Dragon's existing stages and Kolb's Experiential Learning Cycle — that resemblance is worth recording as a resonance and a piece of confirming evidence, the same way amplification treats a mythological parallel to a poem's image. It is not evidence that the general framework is what generated, or should govern, the Figure's own structure. The test of whether a stage belongs is never "does this match an established model." It is whether the stage follows the movement the specific poem and image actually make.

---

## The Production Sequence

Every Figure workshop — Dragon's development from here forward, and every Figure after it — follows this order. No step is skipped, and no step is front-loaded ahead of the one before it:

1. **Research the symbol.** The work above: poem, language, cultural/literary association, human pattern.
2. **Research the facilitation method.** How this kind of material is actually best led — method suited to this specific symbolic field, not generic workshop technique.
3. **Understand the poem/image.** Arrive at a real, defensible reading — an actual understanding of what the piece does and says, not a mood.
4. **Establish the workshop purpose.** What this particular workshop, for this particular Figure, is actually for — named plainly, not inferred backward from whatever deliverables follow.
5. **Script.** The facilitator's actual words and movements, built from steps 1–4.
6. **Visual direction.** What participants see, and why each image is there — in service of the script, not decorative.
7. **Participant materials.** Anything a participant holds, writes in, or takes away.
8. **Deck.** The projected/on-screen artifact — built last, from everything above, not first.
9. **Test.** A live or simulated run, checked against the purpose named in step 4.

## Standing Relationship to Other Governing Documents

This standard governs the *front end* of Figure development — what must happen before script or slide work begins. It does not replace the New AwakenArts Paradigm's ten-stage workshop arc or `AwakenArts_Editorial_Philosophy.md`'s reader-journey model; it supplies the researched substance those structures are built from.

Where a Figure's current materials were produced without this sequence — Dragon's governing document and live presentation were developed through revision passes on an existing Reader sequence, not from documented symbolic-field research — that is a known gap, not a retroactive violation. Whether and when to bring Dragon's materials into conformance with this standard is Susan's decision. This document does not itself authorize that rework.
