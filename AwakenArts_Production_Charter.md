## Purpose

This document establishes the guiding philosophy, editorial standards, production priorities, and development direction for AwakenArts. It serves as the primary reference for all future work, whether performed by Susan Shepler, AI collaborators, designers, developers, or editors.

When questions arise about priorities, structure, or direction, this document takes precedence over individual conversations.

---

## I. Mission

AwakenArts exists to help people recognize symbolic language through image, poem, and thoughtful reflection.

Rather than explaining meaning, AwakenArts creates experiences through which meaning is discovered.

The work invites recognition rather than instruction, reflection rather than prescription, and participation rather than observation.

Christ remains the spiritual center of the work. Scripture remains its authority. The artistic work serves as an invitation toward deeper recognition.

---

## II. The AwakenArts Model

AwakenArts is not simply a publishing project.

It is a creative ecosystem.

Each part exists to invite a deeper level of participation.

Visitors should never feel they have reached the end.

Every experience should naturally lead to another.

---

## III. Core Development Areas

### A. Teach

Develop workshops built around individual Editions.

Outputs include:

- Workshops
- Slide presentations
- Facilitator Guides
- Participant Workbooks

Teaching is one of the primary expressions of AwakenArts.

### B. Publish

Create complete artistic works.

Products include:

- Figure Editions
- Companion Guides
- Seek & Find Journals

Each Edition should possess enough richness to support:

- individual reflection
- guided discussion
- workshop facilitation

### C. Collect

Offer artwork people can live with.

Products include:

- Fine Art Prints
- Greeting Cards
- Future collectible artwork

These are not secondary products.

They represent another way people participate in AwakenArts.

### D. Invite

Create welcoming entry points.

Examples include:

- Explore the Path
- The Path
- Gallery
- Monthly Symbol
- Free Journal

Their purpose is to build confidence and curiosity.

They should never replace the experience of an Edition.

### E. Continue

Support ongoing involvement.

Examples include:

- Newsletter
- Workshop follow-up
- New releases
- Community engagement

AwakenArts is intended to become a continuing relationship rather than a one-time purchase.

---

## IV. Website Philosophy

The website is not merely a catalog.

It functions simultaneously as:

- Gallery
- Studio
- Publishing House
- Store
- Workshop Center
- Learning Environment

Every visitor should immediately recognize that AwakenArts is an active body of creative work.

---

## V. Content Architecture

Every level has a distinct responsibility.

### Homepage

Invite exploration. Generate curiosity. Present AwakenArts as substantial and active.

### The Path

Teach the AwakenArts approach. Explain symbolic recognition. Prepare visitors to engage more deeply.

It is not intended to be a free collection of poems.

### Gallery

Present artwork that inspires exploration.

The Gallery should encourage visitors to discover Editions and print products.

### Editions

Provide complete artistic experiences.

The fullest expression of the image-poem relationship belongs here.

No introductory material should replace the value of an Edition.

### Companion Guides

Expand understanding. Support teaching. Support continued reflection.

### Workshops

Provide guided experiences using:

- images
- poems
- discussion
- writing
- recognition

Workshops are experiences—not lectures.

### Seek & Find Journals

Support ongoing practice. Encourage continued engagement beyond workshops and Editions.

### Prints & Greeting Cards

Extend AwakenArts into everyday life.

Artwork should remain accessible through products that people enjoy displaying, giving, and collecting.

---

## VI. Editorial Principles

Recognition before explanation.

Invite rather than persuade.

Protect mystery.

Allow discovery.

Avoid over-explaining symbolism.

Maintain literary quality.

Maintain artistic quality.

Every published work should feel intentional.

---

## VII. Stewardship of Content

Content should be distributed thoughtfully across the ecosystem.

The value of one product should never unintentionally diminish another.

Therefore, The Path should:

- teach the approach
- establish trust
- invite exploration

It should not provide multiple complete poetic experiences.

Reduce complete poems where appropriate.

Use only enough imagery to teach symbolic recognition.

Preserve the strongest artistic experiences for the Editions.

---

## VIII. Production Standards

Before beginning any significant project, ask:

1. Which development area does this strengthen?
2. Does it invite deeper participation?
3. Does it preserve the uniqueness of the Editions?
4. Does it strengthen future workshops?
5. Does it strengthen AwakenArts as a whole?

If the answer to several of these questions is "no," reconsider the priority.

## Purpose

Commerce at AwakenArts is not merely the exchange of products for payment.

It is the architecture through which visitors discover, experience, collect, learn, and continue participating in the work.

Every platform should support the same mission while serving a distinct role.

---

## I. Commerce Philosophy

Commerce exists to make participation possible.

Every offering should answer one of four questions:

**Discover** — What is AwakenArts?

**Experience** — How do I begin?

**Collect** — What do I want to own?

**Continue** — How do I stay involved?

The objective is not simply to increase sales.

The objective is to deepen participation.

## V. The Monthly Symbol Series

**The Monthly Symbol Series is AwakenArts' principal recurring educational publication, discovery engine, and ongoing public offering.**

It is not a minor recurring feature and not a promotional device for other products. It is the primary way most people will first encounter AwakenArts, and the primary reason they will keep returning. Everything below governs how it works.

### The Audience Promise

The Series exists for anyone curious about symbols, meaning, and pattern in human experience — not only for people already interested in AwakenArts. A reader should be able to discover a single issue with no prior knowledge of AwakenArts, find it genuinely rewarding on its own terms, and only then discover that a larger body of work stands behind it.

### Monthly Editorial Structure

Each issue centers on one broadly interesting symbol.

The symbol is explored through Scripture, literature, art, story, psychology, language, and lived experience — not through a single lens. A symbol is treated as something a reader has already been living alongside, in more places than they realized, before AwakenArts ever named it.

### The Recognition-Without-Prescription Standard

AwakenArts does not prescribe a universal meaning for a symbol; it teaches recognition of recurring patterns while preserving context. This is the same governing principle that shapes the Figure Editions (recognition before explanation, protecting mystery — Section VI of the base Charter), applied here to a teaching format rather than an artistic one. An issue names where a pattern recurs and how different traditions and disciplines have engaged it; it does not flatten those differences into one closed interpretation.

### A Renewable Content Engine

One symbol can generate a Kit issue, a website article, visual posts, a journal prompt, a workshop segment, a short video, and a product connection. The Series is a content engine precisely because a single well-chosen symbol supports many forms without repeating itself — each form does a different job (delivery, archive, discovery, practice, live teaching, commercial connection) around the same editorial core.

The Series is not dependent on new AwakenArts products. It can run indefinitely on the strength of its own editorial premise — the inexhaustible supply of symbols worth this kind of attention — whether or not a new Edition, Companion, or Journal is in production in a given month.

### Two Parallel Publishing Streams

AwakenArts runs two publishing streams side by side, each with a distinct job:

**The educational stream** (the Monthly Symbol Series, and everything it generates per the Renewable Content Engine above) attracts the audience. It begins with broad public interest in symbolic language itself, not with AwakenArts' own catalog.

**The original-works stream** (Figure Editions, Companion Guides, Seek & Find Journals, Workshops, Fine Art Prints, Greeting Cards) provides the signature body of work and the commercial offerings. Editions, Companions, Journals, workshops, prints, and cards become deeper or related expressions of a symbol the Series has already introduced — they are not the subject a given issue is written to sell.

Products may be referenced where genuinely relevant to a symbol under discussion. They are never the reason an issue exists.

### Platform Roles

| Platform | Primary role |
|---|---|
| AwakenArts.com | Permanent home, searchable symbol library, product and workshop hub |
| Kit | Subscription, delivery, sequencing, and ongoing reader relationship |
| Monthly Symbol Series | The named recurring editorial offering — not the platform |
| Commerce platform / store functions | Checkout, payment, product presentation, fulfillment connection |
| Print-on-demand vendors | Production and fulfillment of prints, cards, journals, or books as selected |
| Pinterest and short-form channels | Discovery routes leading to the symbol article and Kit subscription |
| Workshops | Live participation and a bridge into the ongoing Monthly Symbol relationship |

The Monthly Symbol Series is the editorial offering carried across several of these platforms; it is not itself a platform, and no single platform above should be treated as synonymous with it.

### The Website as Archive and Library

The website preserves each issue as part of a growing, searchable symbolic-literacy library. No issue is transient — a reader arriving years after original publication should be able to find, read, and benefit from any past issue as easily as the current one. Over time this library becomes its own reason to visit AwakenArts.com, independent of any single new release.

### Kit: Delivery and Subscriber Sequence

Kit is the delivery and relationship mechanism for the Series specifically (see Section IV): it delivers each issue, sustains the subscriber relationship issue to issue, and carries a reader from first subscription toward the Welcome / Nurture / Invite / Continue sequence already governing Kit's broader role. A subscriber's ongoing relationship with AwakenArts is, in practice, substantially built through their accumulated experience of the Series over time.

### Connections to Workshops and Products

Workshops provide live participation and a bridge into the ongoing Monthly Symbol relationship — a workshop may open with a symbol the Series has already explored, or seed a symbol the Series will take up later. Editions, Companions, Journals, Prints, and Cards connect to the Series as deeper or related expressions of a symbol already introduced, per the Renewable Content Engine and Two Parallel Publishing Streams above — never as the premise of an issue.

### Commerce Pathways and Fulfillment

Commerce platform/store functions handle checkout, payment, and product presentation once a reader chooses to go deeper. Print-on-demand vendors handle production and fulfillment of prints, cards, journals, or books as selected. Pinterest and short-form channels are discovery routes leading a new reader to a symbol article and, from there, to a Kit subscription — they are not sales channels in themselves.

### Governing Sequence

The Series must begin with broad public interest in symbolic language, not with AwakenArts products. Every issue is written first as a genuinely useful piece of symbolic literacy for a general reader; only after that standard is met does it connect, where relevant, to the deeper body of AwakenArts work. Products are deeper expressions and relevant commercial connections — they are not the subject that drives every issue.
